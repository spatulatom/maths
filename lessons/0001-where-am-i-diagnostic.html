Why start with a check?

You said you've probably forgotten even the basics. That may be true, or you may remember more than you think. Guessing would waste your time either way.

These 12 questions cover the four building blocks underneath almost every report you'll build: arithmetic, percentages, averages, and reading a formula. The roadmap shows how they lead into the statistics you'll meet later.

One rule

If you'd be guessing, press Not sure. An honest "Not sure" tells me more than a lucky guess, and nobody is grading you.
