Reference · Math for Data Analysis

The roadmap

What to learn, in what order, and where each piece shows up in Power BI.

Each stage builds on the one before it. We move on once the quizzes show a stage is solid, not when the calendar says so.

Where you are: stages 1–6 are done (every quiz passed). All the remaining lessons are written: stage 7 (0012–0015), the logic lessons (0016–0018), and the wrap-up (0019). A score after a lesson means its quiz is done.

StageTopicsWhere you'll meet it
1 · Number basics Order of operations, negatives, fractions ↔ decimals, rounding, units Every Excel formula and DAX measure
2 · Percentages Percent of, percent change, percentage points, ratios, margin vs markup KPI cards, "% of total", MoM / YoY measures
3 · Averages Mean, median, mode, weighted average, average of averages trap Summaries, AVERAGE vs DIVIDE(SUM, SUM)
4 · Growth over time MoM, YoY, running totals, moving averages, compound growth Time intelligence in DAX, trend visuals
5 · Spread & shape Range, standard deviation, percentiles, quartiles, outliers, distributions Box plots, histograms, data-quality checks
6 · Relationships Correlation, causation, trend lines, slope Scatter charts, Analytics pane, interview questions
7 · Uncertainty (concepts) Probability, sampling, bias, confidence intervals, p-values, A/B tests Reading other people's analyses, interviews
Throughout · Logic Sets (joins), AND/OR/NOT (filters), cardinality (one-to-many) Power Query merges, relationships, CALCULATE
