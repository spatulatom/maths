# Mission: Math for Data Analysis

## Why
Switch careers from web development to data analysis. Get through the Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst Professional Certificate (Coursera), pass the PL-300 exam, and land a junior data analyst job without being caught off guard by the math behind reports, DAX measures, and interview questions.

## Success looks like
- Calculates and explains percent change, percentage points, ratios, and growth (MoM / YoY) without hesitating
- Picks the right average (mean, median, weighted) for a business question and explains why
- Reads a spread (range, standard deviation, quartiles) and spots outliers in a dataset
- Reads a formula like `(new − old) / old` and turns it into an Excel formula or a DAX measure
- Explains correlation vs causation and reads a trend line in plain language
- Enters the Coursera certificate confident that the math is not the bottleneck

## Constraints
- Learned math long ago and has probably forgotten even the basics, so start from the foundations without judgement
- Practises daily: about 1 hour on most days, several hours on some
- Knows Excel (pivot tables, Power Query), SQL concepts, some Python, and strong JavaScript; practice uses Excel first
- Wants "enough to not be caught off guard", not to go deep into the weeds

## Out of scope
- Calculus, linear algebra, formal proofs
- Machine-learning math
- Hand-calculating inferential statistics (concepts only)
